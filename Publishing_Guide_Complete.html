Part 4 — Amazon KDP Account Setup
9
Create your Amazon KDP account
Go to kdp.amazon.com and sign in with your Amazon account (or create one). KDP is free to join — Amazon only takes a royalty cut when you sell a book.
Browser 1. Go to → kdp.amazon.com
2. Click "Sign in" with your Amazon account
3. Complete your Author/Publisher profile:
    • Name: Madhu Sudhana Saddala (or use a pen name)
    • Country: Your country
    • Address: Required for royalty payments
4. Add bank account for royalty payments
    (US accounts: direct deposit; International: bank wire or check)
5. Complete tax interview (W-8BEN for non-US authors)
    → Takes ~10 minutes; required before you can sell
💡
Tax interview tip: If you are outside the US, complete the W-8BEN form to claim a reduced withholding tax rate under your country's US tax treaty. For India, the standard treaty rate is 15% on royalties (vs 30% default). This form is filed digitally inside KDP — no printing required.
10
Understand royalty structures before pricing
Amazon KDP offers two royalty options for Kindle eBooks:
PlanRoyalty ratePrice rangeRestrictions
35% royalty 35% of list price Any price None — can also sell elsewhere (PDF on GitHub is fine)
70% royalty 70% of list price minus delivery cost (~$0.15 for 7MB) $2.99–$9.99 only Must be enrolled in KDP Select (90-day exclusivity for Kindle lending)
ℹ️
Recommended: Price at $9.99 with 70% royalty = $6.85 per sale. Or $14.99 at 35% = $5.25 per sale. The 70% plan earns more per unit but restricts you to $9.99 max and requires KDP Select. Since you're giving away the PDF free on GitHub anyway, KDP Select exclusivity only applies to Kindle Unlimited lending — your free PDF can stay online.

Part 5 — Upload to Amazon KDP
11
Prepare your PDF for KDP (formatting check)
KDP accepts PDF for Kindle and for print. For best Kindle rendering, your PDF should meet these specifications:
PDF Requirements for KDP ✅ Your PDF already meets all these:

Page size: A4 or Letter (your book uses A4 — ✅)
Fonts: Embedded (ReportLab embeds all fonts — ✅)
Images: ≥ 300 DPI (your figures are 300 DPI — ✅)
File size: ≤ 650 MB (your book is 7 MB — ✅)
Colour mode: RGB for eBook, CMYK for print
Bleed: Required for print (add 3mm bleed for paperback)

# For Kindle eBook: your PDF as-is is fine.

15
(Optional) Add a Paperback edition
After your eBook is approved, go back to your KDP dashboard, find the title, and click "Create Paperback". KDP prints on demand — no inventory needed. Paperback royalty is typically 40–60% depending on page count.
Paperback specifications for 200 pages ── Interior ──────────────────────────────────────
Interior type: Black & White (cheaper) or Premium Color
Paper color: White (better for code readability)
Trim size: 6" × 9" (standard academic) or 8.5" × 11"
Bleed: No bleed (for text/code books)

── Cost & Pricing for 200-page B&W ──────────────
Printing cost: ~$3.65 (200pp B&W, 6×9)
Minimum price: $3.65 + Amazon fee ≈ $6.50
Recommended: $24.99 → Royalty ≈ $6.35/sale (40%)
Or try: $34.99 → Royalty ≈ $10.35/sale (60%)
💡
Pro tip: Price the Kindle eBook at $9.99 and the paperback at $29.99. Most academic readers will buy the eBook; the paperback serves readers who prefer physical books and are willing to pay more.

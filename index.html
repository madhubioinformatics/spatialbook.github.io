About the Book

Everything you need to analyse spatial data

From loading SpaceRanger output to training AI foundation models, this book walks through every step of a modern spatial transcriptomics analysis with copy-paste-ready Python and R code.

Built on real public datasets — Allen Brain Atlas, Human Breast Cancer Xenium, and DLPFC Visium — every pipeline is tested and reproducible.

Covers Visium HD, single-molecule platforms, multi-sample integration, deconvolution, ligand-receptor analysis, and clinical applications in oncology.
